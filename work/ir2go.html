The short version

Usability score went from 31.3 to 78.25, and every redesigned screen passed WCAG 2.1 AA.

The problem. IRS2Go was rated 2.8 stars, 45% of people quit before finishing a task, and no screen met WCAG 2.1 AA.

What I did. Mined 350+ reviews and posts, interviewed about 10 taxpayers, and redesigned the 3 tasks people open the app for.

01 · The challenge

An official app people gave up on

People open IRS2Go to check a refund, pay, or find free help. Forms rejected correct details, payments bounced out to the website, and help didn't cover refunds or payments.

03 · Finding the pattern

How usability went from 31 to 78

I analyzed 150+ App Store reviews and 200+ Quora posts, interviewed about 10 taxpayers, ran a survey, and summarized related papers with AI. Then we wrote out every step of each task to see where it broke.

01

Tax Year looked like a text box but was a list.

02

The refund amount had to match to the cent, with no example.

03

Payments sent people out to the website, and some still couldn't pay.

Key insight

Rules you learn by failing

The old form explained its rules only after people got them wrong: Tax Year looked like a text box, and the refund amount had to match to the cent.

Plot twist

Many people quit at the login screen.

They wouldn't log in just to check a refund. "Explore without signing in" became the main button, and nobody dropped off there after the change.

Ideas I dropped

  • Rejected

    Pay straight from the form

    3 of 6 testers hesitated without a summary. A confirmation screen with the total before paying took that to 0 of 6.

04 · The solution

Three tasks that now finish inside the app

Mined 350+ reviews and posts, interviewed about 10 taxpayers, and redesigned the 3 tasks people open the app for.

01 · Solution

Every field explains itself before you type

Each field has a label and an example, and a sample form shows where each answer is on your return, so people don't learn the rules by failing.

✓ Part of the 78.25 redesign

02 · Solution

Your refund reads like a timeline

Received, Approved, Sent replaces a bare "Return Received", so people see where they are and what comes next.

✓ Part of the 78.25 redesign

03 · Solution

Pay without leaving the app

Every payment option and its fee sits side by side in the app, instead of a jump to the website mid-payment.

✓ No trips out to the website
