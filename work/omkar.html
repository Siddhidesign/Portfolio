TL;DR

A clothing-label manufacturer needed its 50+ product catalog built and kept accurate for B2B buyers. 50+ pages shipped. Designers owned navigation and categories; my part was building them faithfully.

What I did

Built the pages one by one from designers' handoff files, about 2 days per page, and checked each against the design before handing it to the lead developers.

  • pages built50+

01 · Problem

Buyers check the website before they pick up the phone

For a clothing-label maker, a broken page or an old listing costs trust with the people about to call.

Buyers check the site before they call, so a broken page or an old listing costs trust.

02 · Solutions

What building it taught me

Solution 01

Raise the mismatch before release

When the design file and the browser didn't match, I raised it early and traced layout bugs in Chrome DevTools, so fixes went to the cause, not the symptom.

Why my handoffs now spell out every state
