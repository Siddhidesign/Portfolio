Where I learned what a handoff leaves out

Built every page from designers' handoff files and checked each against the design before release.

Role
Junior Front-End Developer
Timeline
May 2022 to Jan 2023
Team
Designers and 3 developers
Stack

The short version

50+ pages shipped. Designers owned navigation and categories; my part was building them faithfully.

The problem. A clothing-label manufacturer needed its 50+ product catalog built and kept accurate for B2B buyers.

What I did. Built the pages one by one from designers' handoff files, about 2 days per page, and checked each against the design before handing it to the lead developers.

01 · The challenge

Buyers check the website before they pick up the phone

Buyers check the site before they call, so a broken page or an old listing costs trust.

02 · The solution

What building it taught me

Built the pages one by one from designers' handoff files, about 2 days per page, and checked each against the design before handing it to the lead developers.

01 · Solution

Raise the mismatch before release

When the design file and the browser didn't match, I raised it early and traced layout bugs in Chrome DevTools, so fixes went to the cause, not the symptom.

✓ Why my handoffs now spell out every state

03 · The impact

Every designer should build something

I know which questions a handoff leaves open, because I used to be the one asking them.

Result

50+ catalog pages built, about 2 days each
