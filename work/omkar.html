Omkar Labels · 2022 · Front-end development

Where I learned what a handoff leaves out

Role
Junior Front-End Developer
Timeline
May 2022 to Jan 2023
Team
Designers and 3 developers
Stack
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, jQuery, Bootstrap, WordPress
Problem
A clothing-label manufacturer needed its 50+ product catalog built and kept accurate for B2B buyers.
What I did
Built the pages one by one from designers' handoff files, about 2 days per page, and checked each against the design before handing it to the lead developers.
Result
50+ pages shipped. Designers owned navigation and categories; my part was building them faithfully.
  • 50+pages built
  • ~2 daysper page
  • 8 monthson the developer side of handoffs

01The problem

Buyers check the site before they call, so a broken page or an old listing costs trust.

02Key decisions

1Raise the mismatch before release

WhyWhen the design file and the browser didn't match, I raised it early and traced layout bugs in Chrome DevTools instead of patching symptoms.

✓ Why my handoffs now spell out every state
