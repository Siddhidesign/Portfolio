FindMe · 2025 · Design system · Startup

A design system, built inside the sprint

Role
UX/UI Designer
Timeline
May to Aug 2025
Team
Engineering, product, Marketing
Where
New York
Problem
New screens shipped every sprint, and small drift crept in: a slightly different button, the same pattern solved twice.
What I did
Grew the Figma library to 25+ components across 2 products and designed features from research to final UI, inside the sprint.
Result
28% more engagement on brand pages, and handoffs that named every component and state.
  • 25+components
  • 2products kept consistent
  • 28%more engagement on brand pages
  • 12+interviews and usability tests

FindMe's library is private, so this page describes the method rather than showing it.

01The problem

Drift slows engineers down and makes a product feel stitched together. FindMe was growing faster than its system.

02Research

I supported 12+ user interviews and usability tests in Maze, tagged findings in Dovetail, and fed them straight into the wireframes.

03Key decisions

1Name components the way engineers search for them

WhyA library engineers can't navigate doesn't get used, so every name follows one pattern: Button / Primary / Medium / Default.

✓ Every handoff used library names

2Define every variant and state

WhyWhen nothing is left to guess, built screens match designed ones. Default, hover, focus, disabled and loading are all defined.

✓ Worked with developers until built matched designed

3Design inside the sprint, not ahead of it

WhyJoining backlog refinement and sprint planning meant design questions came up while they were still cheap to answer.

04If I took it further

Ideas, not built

  • AI that flags components used off-spec in Figma before they reach handoff.
