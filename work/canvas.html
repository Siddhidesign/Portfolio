01 · Problem

Students were learning a version of UX that was already changing

The UX curriculum was built before AI, and it fell short of how UX is practiced now.

AI changed how UX research and design are done, but the coursework still reflected the field before it. Students were learning a version of the job that was already changing.

02 · Solutions

How the research reached the syllabus

Solution 01

Research and student feedback on one board

Findings and feedback went onto shared Figma affinity boards across 2 cohorts, so real patterns stood out from one-off complaints.

Solution 02

Consolidate weekly, recommend monthly

I consolidated findings with 10+ research assistants every week and presented recommendations to faculty every month, so research turned into decisions.

Over 2 semesters I reviewed 50+ papers and industry reports with UM-GPT, the university's AI tool, and gathered feedback from 43 students across 8 presentations in 2 cohorts.

Weekly synthesis and monthly recommendations kept findings moving into decisions.
