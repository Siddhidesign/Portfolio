01The problem

AI changed how UX research and design are done, but the coursework still reflected the field before it. Students were learning a version of the job that was already changing.

02Research

Over 2 semesters I reviewed 50+ papers and industry reports with UM-GPT, the university's AI tool, and gathered feedback from 43 students across 8 presentations in 2 cohorts.

03Key decisions

1Put research and student feedback on one board

WhyMapping findings and feedback onto shared Figma affinity boards across 2 cohorts showed real patterns, not one-off complaints.

2Consolidate weekly, recommend monthly

WhyI consolidated findings with 10+ research assistants in weekly Zoom sessions and presented recommendations to faculty every month, so research turned into decisions.

✓ 2 new UX courses
