The problem

AI synthesis can't tell you who said it

The usual AI research workflow is copy and paste. The model only sees what you happened to paste, and the output carries no citations.

The idea

The fix, in one line

Built an MCP server that lets an assistant query a research repository directly, with 5 tools.

Goals

What success had to look like

01

Traceable

Every excerpt carries a participant ID, round and timestamp.

02

Honest counts

Patterns count people, not quotes.

03

Humble

It says what the data can't support.

Who it’s for

The people on the other side of the screen

  • UX researchers using AI to make sense of interviews

Design

Five tools, one constraint: retrieve and count, never interpret

01

Count people, not quotes

pattern_frequency counts distinct participants, because three quotes from one person is not a pattern.

✓ Patterns you can defend

02

Say what the data can't support

draft_findings ends with a NOT SUPPORTED section that refuses causal claims on a small sample.

✓ No overclaiming
