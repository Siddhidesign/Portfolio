01The problem

The usual AI research workflow is copy and paste. The model only sees what you happened to paste, and the output carries no citations.

02Key decisions

1Count people, not quotes

Whypattern_frequency counts distinct participants, because three quotes from one person is not a pattern.

✓ Patterns you can defend

2Say what the data can't support

Whydraft_findings ends with a NOT SUPPORTED section that refuses causal claims on a small sample.

✓ No overclaiming

03What I rejected, and why

  • Rejected

    Letting the AI write the findings

    It would blur who said what. The server retrieves and counts. The researcher still writes the finding.
