How I work

Every project, the same order.

  1. 01

    Go where the work happens

    Store floors, phone calls, real screens. I watch the task before I redraw it.

  2. 02

    Let AI do the heavy reading

    Thousands of reviews, transcripts and papers, sorted into themes in hours.

  3. 03

    Check every finding myself

    Every AI theme goes back to the raw quotes before it becomes a decision.

  4. 04

    Measure what the team feels

    Orders a day, errors, task success. Numbers people already care about.
