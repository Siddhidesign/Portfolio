Questions recruiters ask me

tap a question to see my answer

What kind of role are you looking for?

A UX role where research shapes the product, ideally in fintech, the public sector or B2B tools with real accessibility needs. I'm open to work across the US.

What's your design process?

Research before pixels: interviews and audits first, then sketches, wireframes and a prototype, then usability testing, usually two rounds. I treat every misclick as a design question, not a user error.

How do you use AI in your work?

I use Claude for research synthesis, early exploration and documentation, and I check its themes against the raw quotes by hand. I don't use it for final visual decisions, information architecture, or deciding what a user should be told.

How do you work with developers?

I come from engineering and know HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so my handoffs come with specs, tokens and states, and I can talk through implementation on the developers' terms.
