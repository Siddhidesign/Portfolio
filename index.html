About me

Engineer first, designer now.

I started as an engineer, building websites in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. At Jai Hind Group, I designed the internal order platform that replaced 5 systems, and the component library 3 designers and a PM designed against. Then I earned my master's in Human-Centered Design and Engineering at the University of Michigan-Dearborn.

Today I turn business needs into user flows, wireframes and polished UI, keep screens on brand through a shared design system, and work with developers until the design holds up in production. I explain the reasoning in plain words, so people who aren't designers can weigh in.

I care about the quiet stuff: clear labels, readable color, and the one button you actually need.

A quick redesign of Magic Apron

I watched a first-time DIYer ask The Home Depot's AI assistant, Magic Apron, how to hang a photo frame. In Store Mode they had to ask twice to get the right item, and an empty shelf ended in "Thanks for letting us know." My redesign asks the 2 questions that matter as taps, and finishes the task when the shelf is empty.
