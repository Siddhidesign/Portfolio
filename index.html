About me

Engineer first, designer now.

I started as an engineer, building websites in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. At Jai Hind Group, I designed a retail chain's public website and the order platform its staff use. Then I earned my master's in Human-Centered Design and Engineering at the University of Michigan-Dearborn.

I care about the quiet stuff: clear labels, readable color, and the one button you actually need.
