How I work

  1. 01

    Research

    I go where the work happens and listen to the people doing it.

  2. 02

    Define

    I group what I heard into patterns, then name the problem.

  3. 03

    Ideate

    Many ideas on paper, then the layout in gray.

  4. 04

    Design

    The brand becomes a design system, then polished screens.

  5. 05

    Test

    I watch people use it, then fix what fails.

  6. 06

    Ship and measure

    I work with developers until it holds up, then measure.

About

I started as an engineer building websites, then helped design an order platform at Jai Hind Group that replaced 5 systems. After a master's in Human-Centered Design and Engineering, I start every project with research, and design for the people a business serves and the staff who run it.

I care about the quiet stuff: clear labels, readable color, and the one button you actually need.
