About me

Engineer first, designer now.

I started as an engineer, building websites in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. At Jai Hind Group, a menswear retailer, I was one of 3 designers on the order platform that replaced 5 systems. I owned one of its 5 workflows, from supplier to customer, and built the templates and components the team designed with. Then I earned my master's in Human-Centered Design and Engineering at the University of Michigan-Dearborn.

Today I turn business needs into user flows, wireframes and polished UI, for the people a business serves and the staff who run it. I keep every screen on brand through a shared design system and work with developers until the design holds up in production. At FindMe, I also worked with Marketing on its brand pages. I explain the reasoning in plain words, so people who aren't designers can weigh in.

I care about the quiet stuff: clear labels, readable color, and the one button you actually need.
