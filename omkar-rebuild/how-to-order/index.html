The process

Five Steps From Enquiry to Delivery.

One team looks after your order from the first conversation to dispatch.

  1. 1
    Enquiry & order intakeShare your requirements. Our dedicated team helps you understand them and select the most suitable solution.
  2. 2
    Design & developmentOur skilled designers collaborate with you to create impactful artwork that aligns with your brand identity.
  3. 3
    Artwork approvalYou approve the artwork and specifications, and production is matched to exactly what you approved.
  4. 4
    Production & 4QCPrinting and finishing on our flexo, offset and digital machines, with four quality checks along the way.
  5. 5
    DeliveryReliable logistics partners deliver your order within the agreed timeframe.

Your enquiry checklist

  • Product type and quantity
  • Size and preferred material
  • Artwork, logo files or brand guidelines
  • Care symbols and fibre content, for wash care labels
  • Delivery location and required date

Don’t have everything yet? Send what you have and our team will help with the rest.

Quality on every order

Four Checks Before It Ships.

Our 4-point Quality Check System runs on every order, from raw material to packing.

  1. 01Inward material checkRaw materials undergo rigorous inspection to ensure they meet our stringent standards.
  2. 02In-process checkDesigns are checked at every production stage and matched to approved artworks and customer specifications.
  3. 03Finished goods checkFinal products are thoroughly examined for adherence to specifications and defect-free output.
  4. 04Packaging checkWe make sure every order is packed securely and damage-free before dispatch.
