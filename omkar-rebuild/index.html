Our machines

Advanced Printing & Finishing Equipment

We invest in cutting-edge printing technology for consistent, high-quality output. Our plants house flexographic and digital printers and precision cutting equipment, including:

  • Flexo label printing. Stable, high-speed printing on cloth, ribbon and paper rolls, with a thick, solid ink layer and good fastness. Two-side tension control keeps every run accurate.
  • Ultrasonic label cutting. Ultra-smooth, perfectly sealed edges on printed and woven labels, with a PLC touchscreen and intelligent mark sensor to prevent errors at speed.
  • Heidelberg Speedmaster® offset. High-productivity offset printing at up to 30,000 sheets per day, easy to operate and integrated into our workflow.

How we work

More Than a Label Printer.
A Partner in Quality.

Every order follows the same path and passes our four-point Quality Check System (4QC) before it is dispatched.

  1. 1
    Order intakeOur dedicated team helps you understand your requirements and select the most suitable solution.
  2. 2
    Design & developmentOur designers collaborate with you to create impactful artwork that aligns with your brand identity.
  3. 3
    4QC systemEvery job passes our robust four-point Quality Check System before it is released.
  4. 4
    DeliveryReliable logistics partners deliver your order within the agreed timeframe.

Inside 4QC

  1. 01Inward material checkRaw materials undergo rigorous inspection to ensure they meet our stringent standards.
  2. 02In-process checkDesigns are checked at every production stage and matched to approved artworks and customer specifications.
  3. 03Finished goods checkFinal products are thoroughly examined for adherence to specifications and defect-free output.
  4. 04Packaging checkWe make sure every order is packed securely and damage-free before dispatch.
