FAQs

Questions About Our Products

Which materials can wash care labels be printed on?

Cotton, recycled satin, taffeta and DuPont™ Tyvek®. Our team will help you choose the most suitable one for your product.

Do you offer recycled options?

Yes. We print wash care labels on recycled satin and offer recycled polyester tapes, and our production is certified to the Global Recycled Standard.

Can you design the artwork for us?

Yes. Our in-house designers work with you to create artwork that aligns with your brand identity, and production is matched to the artwork you approve.
